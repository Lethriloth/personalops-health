Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026.

PersonalOps Brief is a personal tool used only by its developer. No one else can sign up, and no one else's data is collected.

What it accesses

With the account owner's consent, it reads sleep data from Google Health through the Google Health API, using the read-only scope googlehealth.sleep.readonly. It requests no other Google Health data, and it never writes to Google Health.

How the data is used

Each morning, the previous night's sleep data is used to write a short brief, which is emailed to the account owner. The brief is written by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude), running as a scheduled task on the owner's own account. The data is used for nothing else.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth credentials are stored privately and are accessible only to the owner. Sleep data is not stored by this tool beyond the brief email itself. It is not sold or shared, and it is not used for advertising or to train models.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
